# Style presets

Named styles as points on the seven axes of `SKILL.md`, in the order depth · translucency · expressiveness · formality · ornament · density · motion.
The coordinates are this repository's starting points, not measured values, and the levers other than the shadow override have not been tested in this stack.
A preset is where you start; the brief records which axes moved and why.

| Preset            | Axes                      | Route when                                                    | Avoid when                               | Accessibility risk                                       | Levers                                                                                                 | Watch for                                                                                                 |
| ----------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `swiss`           | 0 · 0 · 1 · 3 · 0 · 1 · 0 | enterprise, documentation, SaaS, finance, legal               | playful consumer brands                  | low                                                      | `--radius` 0 to 0.25rem, no resting shadow, strict grid, one strong display face                       | closest to the shadcn default, so the departure must come from the display face or color energy           |
| `editorial`       | 0 · 0 · 1 · 2 · 1 · 1 · 0 | news, long-form, newsletters, studio and portfolio sites      | dashboards, catalogues                   | low                                                      | serif `--font-heading-family`, narrow measure, generous leading                                        | broadsheet hairlines and cream + serif + terracotta are 2026 tells (`tells.md`)                           |
| `flat-expressive` | 1 · 0 · 2 · 1 · 1 · 1 · 1 | cross-platform consumer apps, MVPs, low-density product pages | dense data with weak signifiers          | low when controls keep visible fills                     | higher brand chroma, larger `--radius`, bold scale contrast                                            | flat controls without fills fail the density gate                                                         |

## Modifiers, not presets

- **Glass accent:** translucency 2 on overlays, menus and sticky chrome over rich media, combined with any preset that allows it under the gates. Glass applied to whole landing pages is an AI-default look.

## Out of scope here

- **Neumorphism:** excluded by the hard gate in `SKILL.md`.
- **Liquid Glass:** Apple's platform material has no web content-layer equivalent; the community skills found target SwiftUI, Compose and Flutter.
- **Maximalism and retro or Y2K palettes:** they need several hues and conflict with `ui-design`'s one-brand-hue rule. Use them only when the user asks and explicitly waives that rule, and record the waiver in the brief.
